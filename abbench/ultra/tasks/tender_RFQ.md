# Request for Quotation RFQ-2026-114 — Research Team Design Advisory

Issued by: Northbank Applied AI Institute (fictional buyer for this exercise)

Northbank is seeking an adviser to design and staff the teams that run its machine-learning experiments. Respondents must answer every question below. Answers must be supported by evidence; do not claim anything you cannot evidence. Where you cannot evidence something, say so plainly and state what you would provide.

Q1. Identify your organisation: legal entity name, trading name, ABN and GST registration status.
Q2. Describe a comparable engagement you have delivered or proposed: client type, scope, deliverables and duration.
Q3. Describe your method and the evidence base it draws on, with quantities where you have them.
Q4. Provide a fixed price for a comparable three-milestone engagement, showing GST and how payments are split across milestones.
Q5. State your position on using psychometric or strengths assessments in recruitment or performance decisions.
Q6. Describe your accreditation or certification with any assessment publisher.
Q7. How would the engagement support an R&D Tax Incentive claim, and what records would you keep and for how long?
Q8. State your professional indemnity insurance cover amount and policy number.
Q9. List three government clients you have delivered for in the past five years, with contract values.
