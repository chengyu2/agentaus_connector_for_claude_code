ultracode: This folder holds Australian R&D Tax Incentive data across several files:
- rdti_2022_23.xlsx - 2022-23 claims (ABN printed with spaces)
- rdti_2021_22_late.xlsx - late lodgers for the 2021-22 income year
- amendments.csv - amended expenditure figures for claims in either income year
- sector_watchlist.xlsx - 60 companies our team tracks, with a sector each

Rules: identify a company by its 11-digit ABN; where a row has no ABN, identify it by company name. A company's "effective" expenditure for a year is its amended figure for that year if amendments.csv has one (even if that figure is 0), otherwise its reported figure.

Answer these and write answers.json with exactly these keys:
- q1_companies_2022_23: number of distinct companies with a 2022-23 claim
- q2_total_effective_2022_23: total effective 2022-23 expenditure (integer dollars)
- q3_median_effective_2022_23: median effective 2022-23 expenditure per company, rounded to the nearest dollar
- q4_count_at_least_10m: number of companies whose effective 2022-23 expenditure is at least $10,000,000
- q5_top_abn: ABN (11 digits, no spaces, as a string) of the company with the largest effective 2022-23 expenditure
- q6_sector_totals: object mapping each watchlist sector to the total effective 2022-23 expenditure of its watchlist companies
- q7_in_both_years: number of ABNs that appear both in the 2022-23 claims and the 2021-22 late lodgers
- q8_net_amendment_change_2022_23: sum over 2022-23 amendments of (amended figure minus reported figure)

Cross-check every number before writing the file, then summarise the findings briefly.
